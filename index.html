📖 CORE MOVES REFERENCE

STRIKE A POSE

Trigger: Give self/ally positive status or make an impression

ON A HIT (7+): Gain Juice = Power (min 2 on 10+). Spend Juice to create story tags (1 each) or statuses (1 per tier).

ON A 10+: Also buy upgrades (1 Juice each): ⏰ Prolong effect (ongoing), 📏 Scale up, ✨ Make flashier

ON A MISS (6-): MC makes a hard move

SLAY

Trigger: Give opponent status or overcome Ignorance Limit

ON A HIT (7+): Give them status with tier = Power

ON A 10+: Choose TWO upgrades (can pick same twice)

ON A 7-9: Choose ONE upgrade

ON A MISS (6-): MC makes a hard move

Upgrades: ⚡ Killing it! (no counterattack/concessions) • 💪 More power (+1 tier) • 👥 More coverage (small group) • 🎯 Take something (remove/get story tag) • 👀 Keep focused • 🔄 Make comeback (reduce own negative -1)

GET A CLUE

Trigger: Investigate, research, gather information

ON A HIT (7+): Get Clues = Power. Spend each to ask MC/player a question (honest, not necessarily direct)

ON A 7-9: MC also picks ONE complication: 🔄 Counter Question • 😰 Side Effects (tier-1 status, cannot Resist) • 🎭 Drama

ON A MISS (6-): MC makes a hard move

TALK IT OUT

Trigger: De-escalate or negotiate in good faith

ON A HIT (7+): Choose ONE: 🎯 Make progress • 🤝 Strike a deal • 💕 Bond (relationship status, tier = Power)

ON A 7-9: MC/target also picks ONE: 💰 Condition/Price • 👁️ Show Understanding • 💔 Get Attached (you receive relationship status)

ON A MISS (6-): They put up walls / MC makes a hard move

CARE

Trigger: Help with emotional/physical needs

ON A HIT (7+): Remove story tags or status tiers = Power (you distribute)

ON A 7-9: MC gives you tier-1 negative status (concerned, tired, saddened, dirty, drained). Cannot Resist.

ON A MISS (6-): Care backfires / MC makes a hard move

RESIST

Trigger: Avoid status being imposed on you

ON A 10+: Fend off effect - take NO status

ON A 7-9: Take status with one less tier (e.g., Guilty-3 → Guilty-2)

ON A MISS (6-): Take full status

Note: Cannot Resist side effects from Care (7-9), Get a Clue (7-9), or Be Vulnerable (7-9)

BE VULNERABLE

Trigger: Put yourself on the line - action that could end badly

ON A 10+: You do it and it's wonderful!

ON A 7-9: You do it, but MC picks ONE: 😰 Side Effects (negative status, cannot Resist) • 🔥 Burnout (one tag burnt) • 🎭 Drama

ON A MISS (6-): You fail OR succeed but MC makes hard move
